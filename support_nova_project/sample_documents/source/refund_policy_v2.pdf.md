# Lumora Home Technologies - Refund Policy

Document ID: REF-POL | Version: 2.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: REFUND

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains when Lumora Home Technologies refunds customers for devices and services bought from the Lumora online store. It replaces Refund Policy version 1.0.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

### 2.1 Return Window

Customers may request a refund within 30 calendar days of delivery.

### 2.2 Condition of Product

Unopened devices receive a full refund. Opened devices in working condition receive a full refund minus a 10 percent restocking fee.

### 2.3 Dead on Arrival and Transit Damage

Devices that are dead on arrival or damaged in transit receive a full refund including shipping charges, with no restocking fee, when reported within 7 days of delivery.

## 3. Refund Processing

### 3.1 Processing Time

Approved refunds are issued within 7 business days after the returned device is received and inspected at the Lumora returns warehouse.

### 3.2 Payment Method

Refunds are always issued to the original payment method. Refunds to a different card or account are not permitted.

### 3.3 Non-refundable Items

Professional installation fees are not refundable once the installation has been completed. LumoraCare+ subscription fees are refunded pro-rata for unused full months only.

## 4. Agent Rules

### 4.1 No Promise Before Inspection

Agents must not promise or guarantee a refund before the returned device has been inspected, except for confirmed transit damage under section 2.3.

### 4.2 Policy Exceptions

Refunds outside the 30-day window are policy exceptions and require approval from a Customer Relations manager. Agents must not approve exceptions themselves.
