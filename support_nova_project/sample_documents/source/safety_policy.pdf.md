# Lumora Home Technologies - Product Safety and Recall Policy

Document ID: SAF-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: SAFETY

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every report of a possible safety hazard involving a Lumora device, including overheating, burning smell, smoke, fire, sparks, melting parts or electric shock.

## 2. Classification

### 2.1 Safety Reports Are Always Critical

Every safety report is classified as Critical urgency and priority P0, regardless of how calmly or politely the complaint is written and regardless of the value of the device.

### 2.2 Mandatory Escalation

Safety reports must be escalated to the Product Safety specialist team within 1 hour of receipt. Reports of smoke or fire are also escalated to Critical Management.

### 2.3 Injuries and Children

If anyone was injured, or a child was involved, the case is escalated to Critical Management Escalation immediately.

## 3. Customer Instructions

### 3.1 Immediate Advice

The agent must advise the customer to stop using the device immediately and to unplug it or switch off power at the breaker if it is safe to do so.

### 3.2 Prohibited Actions

Agents must not ask the customer to troubleshoot, open or repair the device. Agents must not admit liability or promise compensation. Only the Product Safety team may offer compensation.

## 4. Recalls

### 4.1 Recall Check

The Product Safety team checks whether the device serial number is part of an active recall and informs the customer of the recall remedy.
