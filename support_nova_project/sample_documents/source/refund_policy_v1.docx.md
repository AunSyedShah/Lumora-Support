# Lumora Home Technologies - Refund Policy

Document ID: REF-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: REFUND

Effective Date: 2025-01-01

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains when Lumora Home Technologies refunds customers for devices and services bought from the Lumora online store.

## 2. Refund Eligibility

### 2.1 Return Window

Customers may request a refund within 14 calendar days of delivery.

### 2.2 Condition of Product

The device must be returned unused, in its original packaging, with all accessories.

## 3. Refund Processing

### 3.1 Processing Time

Approved refunds are issued within 10 business days to the original payment method.

### 3.2 Non-refundable Items

Professional installation fees are not refundable once the installation has been completed.
