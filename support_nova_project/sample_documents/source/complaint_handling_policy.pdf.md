# Lumora Home Technologies - Complaint Handling Policy

Document ID: CMP-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: SERVICE_QUALITY

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the standards every Lumora team follows when receiving, handling and closing customer complaints.

## 2. Receiving Complaints

### 2.1 Complaint Channels

Customers may complain through the web form, e-mail, live chat or by uploading a written complaint. All channels are handled in the same queue.

### 2.2 Acknowledgement

Every complaint is acknowledged within the response time defined for its priority in the Service Level Agreement (SLA-POL).

## 3. Communication

### 3.1 Communication Standards

Each complaint has one named contact. The customer receives an update at least every 2 business days until the complaint is closed.

### 3.2 Business Customers

Business customers are given a dedicated business account contact for every complaint.

## 4. Repeat and Unresolved Complaints

### 4.1 Repeat Complaints

A new complaint about an issue the customer has raised before must be linked to the earlier complaint.

### 4.2 Unresolved Complaints

When a customer says a previous complaint was not resolved, the case goes to Supervisor Review. The supervisor reviews why the first resolution failed.

### 4.3 Third Complaint on the Same Issue

A third complaint about the same unresolved issue is escalated to the Department Manager.

## 5. Staff Behaviour

### 5.1 Behaviour Complaints

Complaints about staff behaviour are acknowledged with an apology. The staff member, date and channel are recorded and the case is forwarded to the team supervisor.

### 5.2 Serious Misconduct

Complaints about threats, harassment, discrimination or abuse by staff are escalated to the Department Manager.

## 6. Prohibited Conduct

### 6.1 Prohibited Conduct

Staff must never blame the customer, share another customer's information or close a complaint without the customer's confirmation.
