# Lumora Home Technologies - LumoraCare+ Subscription Terms

Document ID: SUB-TERMS | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: SUBSCRIPTION

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

These terms apply to the LumoraCare+ subscription.

## 2. Plans

### 2.1 Plans and Features

LumoraCare+ is available as a monthly or yearly plan. It includes 30 days of cloud video recording for all cameras and extended device protection for up to 36 months from purchase.

## 3. Plan Changes

### 3.1 Upgrades

An upgrade takes effect immediately. The customer pays a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period.

### 3.2 Downgrades

A downgrade takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle. No partial refund is given for the current period.

## 4. Renewals

### 4.1 Renewal Reminders

Lumora sends a renewal reminder e-mail 7 days before every yearly renewal. If no reminder was sent, the customer may request a refund of the renewal.

### 4.2 Renewal Refunds

A renewal charge is refunded in full if the customer asks within 14 days of the renewal charge.

### 4.3 Late Renewal Requests

After 14 days the renewal is not refunded. The customer can cancel, and the subscription then ends at the end of the current term.

## 5. Service Availability

### 5.1 Feature Availability

Cloud recording works only while the subscription is active and recording is enabled for the camera in the Lumora app.

### 5.2 Service Outage Credit

If a LumoraCare+ feature is unavailable because of a Lumora outage lasting more than 24 hours, the customer receives one month of subscription credit. Cash compensation is not offered.
