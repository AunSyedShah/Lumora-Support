# Lumora Home Technologies - Return and Replacement Policy

Document ID: RET-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: PRODUCT_DEFECT

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Returns

### 1.1 Return Window

Devices may be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery. Refund amounts follow the Refund Policy (REF-POL).

## 2. Faulty or Incomplete Deliveries

### 2.1 Dead on Arrival

A device that does not power on when first used, reported within 7 days of delivery, is replaced or fully refunded at the customer's choice. Lumora arranges a free return pickup.

### 2.2 Dead on Arrival After 7 Days

A device reported as not working more than 7 days after delivery is handled under the Warranty Policy (WAR-POL).

### 2.3 Missing Parts

Missing parts or accessories reported within 14 days of delivery are shipped free of charge. The customer does not need to return the device.

### 2.4 Missing Parts After 14 Days

Missing parts reported after 14 days can be bought from the Lumora store.

## 3. Wrong Items

### 3.1 Wrong Item

Wrong items are handled under section 4.2 of the Delivery and Shipping Policy (DEL-POL).

## 4. Replacement Limits

### 4.1 Replacement Limits

If a replacement device is also dead on arrival, the customer is offered a full refund instead of a further replacement. Further replacements need Supervisor approval.
