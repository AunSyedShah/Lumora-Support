# Lumora Home Technologies - Service Level Agreement

Document ID: SLA-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: SLA | Status: Active

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Priorities

### 1.1 P0 Critical

First response within 1 hour, resolution within 24 hours. At risk after 50 percent of the resolution time.

### 1.2 P1 High

First response within 4 hours, resolution within 48 hours. At risk after 75 percent of the resolution time.

### 1.3 P2 Medium

First response within 8 hours, resolution within 72 hours. At risk after 75 percent of the resolution time.

### 1.4 P3 Low

First response within 24 hours, resolution within 120 hours. At risk after 80 percent of the resolution time.

## 2. SLA Rules

### 2.1 Measuring Time

SLA time starts when the complaint is submitted. Time spent waiting for the customer to reply is not counted.

### 2.2 At-risk Complaints

Complaints at risk of missing their resolution target are shown to the Department Manager every day.
