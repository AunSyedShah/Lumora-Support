# Lumora Home Technologies - Compliance Guidelines

Document ID: CPL-GDL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Compliance | Status: Active

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

These guidelines apply to every message sent to a customer.

## 2. Customer Communication

### 2.1 No Unsupported Promises

Agents and automated responses must not promise refunds, replacements, compensation or policy exceptions that a policy does not allow.

### 2.2 Timelines

Only timelines written in Lumora policies may be given to customers.

## 3. Legal and Regulatory

### 3.1 Legal Threats

Complaints that mention legal action are escalated to the Department Manager. Agents must not admit liability or discuss the legal case.

### 3.2 Regulators and Data Rights

Complaints that mention a regulator, consumer protection body or a formal data-protection request are escalated for review within 1 business day.

### 3.3 Media and Public Statements

Complaints threatening media or social media exposure are sent to Supervisor Review. Agents must not make public statements.

## 4. Vulnerable Customers

### 4.1 Vulnerable Customers

Customers who mention age, disability, a medical condition or being left without heating are sent to Supervisor Review and handled as High priority.
