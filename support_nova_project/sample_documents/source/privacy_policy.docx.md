# Lumora Home Technologies - Privacy Policy

Document ID: PRV-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: PRIVACY

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

This policy explains how Lumora protects customer data and camera footage.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data We Collect

Lumora stores account details, order history, device settings and, for LumoraCare+ customers, camera recordings.

## 3. Camera Footage

### 3.1 Camera Footage Protection

Camera footage is encrypted and can only be viewed by the account holder and the people they choose to share it with.

## 4. Privacy Incidents

### 4.1 Footage Exposure

If footage may have been seen by someone else, all camera sharing links are disabled, access logs are preserved, and the Privacy team is notified within 1 hour. The case is sent to Compliance Review. Agents must not downplay the exposure or promise compensation.

### 4.2 Customer Notification

Affected customers are informed about a privacy incident within 72 hours.

## 5. Marketing

### 5.1 Marketing Opt-out

Customers can opt out of marketing at any time. Opt-outs are processed within 2 business days.

### 5.2 Marketing After Opt-out

Marketing sent after an opt-out is a compliance failure. The customer is unsubscribed immediately and the case is sent to Compliance Review.
