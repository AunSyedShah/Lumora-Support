# Lumora Home Technologies - Data Retention Policy

Document ID: DRT-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: PRIVACY

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

This policy explains how long customer data is kept and how deletion requests are handled.

## 2. Retention

### 2.1 Retention Periods

Account data is kept while the account is open. Camera recordings are kept for 30 days. Complaint records are kept for 3 years.

## 3. Deletion Requests

### 3.1 Deletion Requests

After the customer's identity is verified, a deletion request is completed within 30 days and the customer receives a confirmation.

### 3.2 Legal Retention Exceptions

Invoices and payment records must be kept for 7 years for tax purposes and are not deleted.

### 3.3 Overdue Deletion Requests

A deletion request not completed within 30 days is sent to Compliance Review and completed as a priority.
