# Lumora Home Technologies - Billing and Payments Policy

Document ID: BIL-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: BILLING

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

This policy covers payments, charges, refunds of incorrect charges, invoices and payment disputes.

## 2. Payments

### 2.1 Accepted Payment Methods

Lumora accepts credit and debit cards and approved digital wallets. Refunds are always made to the original payment method.

## 3. Charge Problems

### 3.1 Duplicate Charges

When a customer reports a duplicate charge, Billing verifies both transactions in the payment system. A confirmed duplicate is refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days.

### 3.2 Incorrect Charges

An incorrect charge is compared with the order invoice. If the charge is wrong, the difference is refunded.

### 3.3 Price Changes

Lumora does not refund the difference when a product price drops after purchase and does not offer price matching.

### 3.4 High-value Disputes

Any charge or refund dispute above 500 USD is reviewed by the Billing Department Manager.

## 4. Invoices

### 4.1 Invoice Corrections

Incorrect invoices are corrected and re-issued within 5 business days.

### 4.2 Business Invoices

Business customers receive corrected VAT invoices within 2 business days.

## 5. Payment Disputes

### 5.1 Chargebacks

If a customer opens a chargeback with their bank, the case is escalated to the Billing Department Manager and handled only by the Billing team.
