# Lumora Home Technologies - Installation Service Policy

Document ID: INS-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: INSTALLATION

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

This policy covers the professional installation service offered for Lumora devices.

## 2. Appointments

### 2.1 Appointment Windows

Installations are booked in 4-hour appointment windows. The technician calls 30 minutes before arrival.

## 3. Missed Appointments

### 3.1 Missed Appointments

If the technician misses an appointment, Lumora apologises and reschedules within 3 business days.

### 3.2 Installation Fee Waiver

When Lumora misses an appointment, the installation fee for that order is waived.

### 3.3 Premium Rebooking

LumoraCare+ customers whose appointment was missed are rebooked within 1 business day.

## 4. Installation Problems

### 4.1 Faulty Installation

If an installation was done incorrectly, Lumora books a free revisit within 5 business days.

### 4.2 Property Damage

Claims for damage to the customer's property during installation are escalated to the Department Manager. Agents must not admit liability or promise compensation.

### 4.3 Electrical Hazards

Exposed wires, sparks or tripping breakers after an installation are safety hazards. The customer is told to switch off power at the breaker and the case goes to Product Safety within 1 hour.
