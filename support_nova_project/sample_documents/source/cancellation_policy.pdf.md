# Lumora Home Technologies - Cancellation Policy

Document ID: CAN-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: SUBSCRIPTION

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

This policy covers the cancellation of device orders and LumoraCare+ subscriptions.

## 2. Order Cancellation

### 2.1 Before Dispatch

An order cancelled before dispatch is refunded in full within 5 business days.

### 2.2 After Dispatch

An order that has already been dispatched cannot be cancelled. The customer can return it under the Return and Replacement Policy.

## 3. Subscription Cancellation

### 3.1 Cancelling LumoraCare+

Customers can cancel LumoraCare+ at any time in the Lumora app or on the website. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period and a confirmation e-mail is sent.

### 3.2 Cancellation Channels

Customers must never be required to phone Lumora in order to cancel. Any agent can process a cancellation request received through any channel.

### 3.3 Charges After Cancellation

Any subscription charge taken after the date the customer cancelled is refunded in full to the original payment method.
