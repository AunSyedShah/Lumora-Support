# Lumora Home Technologies - Warranty Policy

Document ID: WAR-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: WARRANTY

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

This policy covers faults in Lumora devices during and after the warranty period.

## 2. Coverage

### 2.1 Standard Warranty

Every Lumora device has a 12-month warranty from the purchase date. Faults covered by the warranty are repaired, or the device is replaced if repair is not possible.

### 2.2 Firmware Failures

A device that stops working because of a Lumora firmware update is covered by the warranty and replaced.

### 2.3 Out of Warranty

After 12 months the customer is offered a paid repair quote. Free repairs outside warranty are not allowed.

### 2.4 LumoraCare+ Extended Protection

Customers with an active LumoraCare+ subscription are covered for repairs and replacements for up to 36 months from purchase.

## 3. Claims and Repairs

### 3.1 Replacement Assessment

A device is replaced only after a technician confirms that it cannot be repaired.

### 3.2 Claim Review

A customer may ask for a rejected claim to be reviewed once by a technician. A rejection is never overturned without a technician review.

### 3.3 Repair Time

Warranty repairs are completed within 10 business days of the device arriving at the repair centre.

### 3.4 Repair Delays

If a repair takes more than 15 business days, the customer is offered a replacement device.

### 3.5 Replacement Limits

A device can be replaced once per warranty period. A further replacement needs Supervisor approval.

## 4. Exclusions

### 4.1 Exclusions

The warranty does not cover water damage, drops, cracked screens, other physical damage, or devices opened by anyone other than Lumora.
