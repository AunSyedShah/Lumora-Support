# Lumora Home Technologies - Technical Support Guide

Document ID: TEC-SOP | Version: 1.0 | Type: SOP | Status: Active | Category: TECH_SUPPORT

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Purpose

Procedures for agents handling app, connectivity, pairing and firmware problems.

## 2. Connectivity

### 2.1 App Connectivity

Check the Lumora service status page first. Then ask the customer to update the app and restart the hub.

### 2.2 Device Pairing

Confirm the hub and the device are on the same 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network. Reset the device and pair it again.

### 2.3 Service Outages

During a known outage, tell the customer about the outage and the expected fix time. Do not ask the customer to troubleshoot.

### 2.4 Smart Lock Connectivity

An offline Smart Lock is High priority. Confirm the customer can use the physical key or keypad, then restore the connection. Never tell a customer to leave a door unlocked.

### 2.5 Repeated Issues

If the same technical issue is reported a third time, assign a senior technical specialist instead of repeating the basic steps.

## 3. Firmware

### 3.1 Firmware Update Failures

Confirm the current firmware version and guide the customer through a manual update.

### 3.2 Devices Disabled by Updates

If a device stopped working after a Lumora update, it is replaced under section 2.2 of the Warranty Policy.

## 4. Prohibited Guidance

### 4.1 Prohibited Guidance

Never ask a customer to open a device, remove its casing or change its wiring.
