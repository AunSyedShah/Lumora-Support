# Lumora Home Technologies - Account Security Policy

Document ID: SEC-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: ACCOUNT_SECURITY

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

This policy covers account access, lockouts, unauthorized access and unauthorized orders.

## 2. Account Access

### 2.1 Account Lockout

Before unlocking an account, the agent verifies the customer's identity with two factors. The account is then unlocked and a password reset is forced.

### 2.2 Home-access Devices

Customers who own a Smart Lock or Video Doorbell and cannot access their account or home are handled as High priority. After verification, the agent reminds them about the physical key or keypad.

## 3. Security Incidents

### 3.1 Unauthorized Access

If someone else accessed an account, the account is locked immediately, all sessions are revoked, a password reset is forced and the login history is reviewed. The case is sent to Compliance Review.

### 3.2 Unauthorized Orders

An order the customer did not place is cancelled if it has not shipped. The account is secured and the order is refunded after verification.

### 3.3 Credentials

Agents must never ask a customer for their password.
