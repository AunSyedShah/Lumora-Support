# Lumora Home Technologies - Delivery and Shipping Policy

Document ID: DEL-POL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active | Category: DELIVERY

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Delivery Timelines

### 1.1 Standard Delivery

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days from dispatch.

### 1.2 Express Delivery

Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days from dispatch and costs an additional shipping fee. If an express order arrives late by 1 business day or more, the express shipping fee is refunded.

## 2. Delayed Delivery

### 2.1 Investigation

If an order has not arrived by the estimated delivery date, the Logistics team must check the courier tracking status and give the customer an updated delivery estimate within 1 business day.

### 2.2 Delay Compensation

If delivery is more than 5 business days later than the original estimate, the customer is eligible for a refund of the shipping fee. No other compensation is offered for delivery delays.

### 2.3 Long Delays

If delivery is more than 10 business days late, the case is sent to Supervisor Review and the customer may cancel the order for a full refund.

## 3. Lost Parcels

### 3.1 Lost Parcel Declaration

A parcel is declared lost when the courier has shown no tracking update for 7 business days.

### 3.2 Resolution

For a lost parcel the customer may choose a free replacement shipment or a full refund of the order.

### 3.3 Marked as Delivered but Not Received

If tracking shows a parcel as delivered but the customer has not received it, Logistics requests proof of delivery from the courier within 2 business days before any replacement or refund is offered.

## 4. Damaged or Wrong Items

### 4.1 Reporting Damage

Transit damage must be reported within 7 days of delivery with photos of the device and the packaging.

### 4.2 Wrong Item Delivered

If the wrong item is delivered and reported within 14 days, Lumora arranges a free return pickup and ships the correct item at no cost.

### 4.3 Late Reports

Damage reported after 7 days, or a wrong item reported after 14 days, is sent to Supervisor Review. No replacement or refund is guaranteed for late reports.
