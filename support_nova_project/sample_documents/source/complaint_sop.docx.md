# Lumora Home Technologies - Complaint Handling SOP

Document ID: CMP-SOP | Version: 1.0 | Type: SOP | Status: Active

Effective Date: 2025-03-01

## 1. Purpose

Step-by-step procedure for agents handling a new complaint.

## 2. Triage

### 2.1 Triage Steps

Read the complaint, identify the main issue, check the customer's previous complaints, assign the department and priority, and send the acknowledgement.

### 2.2 Refund Requests

Accept refund requests made within 14 days of delivery. Refund requests after 14 days must be declined.

## 3. Escalation

### 3.1 Escalation

Escalate complaints according to the Complaint Escalation Procedure (ESC-SOP).

## 4. Closing

### 4.1 Closing Complaints

Close a complaint only after the customer confirms the resolution or does not reply for 7 days after the final update.
