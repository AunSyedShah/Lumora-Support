# Lumora Home Technologies - Department Routing Rules

Document ID: RTG-RUL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Routing | Status: Active

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. General Rule

Every complaint is routed to one primary department. Departments needed for secondary issues are added as supporting departments.

## 2. Routing by Category

### 2.1 Delivery

Delivery complaints go to Logistics and Delivery.

### 2.2 Billing and Subscription

Billing and subscription complaints go to Billing and Payments. Problems with subscription features go to Technical Support.

### 2.3 Refunds

Refund complaints go to Returns and Refunds.

### 2.4 Product Defects and Warranty

Defects and warranty claims go to Warranty and Repairs. Devices dead on arrival go to Returns and Refunds. Missing parts go to Logistics and Delivery.

### 2.5 Technical Support

App, firmware and pairing problems go to Technical Support.

### 2.6 Installation

Installation complaints go to Installation Services.

### 2.7 Account Security and Privacy

Account problems go to Account Security. Privacy complaints go to Privacy and Compliance.

### 2.8 Safety

All safety complaints go to Product Safety.

### 2.9 Service Quality

Complaints about staff, communication or unresolved complaints go to Customer Relations.

## 3. Management Escalations

### 3.1 Management Escalations

Legal threats, very high-value disputes, injuries and fire are also sent to Management Escalations.
