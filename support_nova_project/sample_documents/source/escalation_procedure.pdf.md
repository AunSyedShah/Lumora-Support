# Lumora Home Technologies - Complaint Escalation Procedure

Document ID: ESC-SOP | Version: 1.0 | Type: SOP | Status: Active

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Escalation Levels

Lumora uses the following escalation levels: No Escalation, Supervisor Review, Department Manager, Specialist Team, Compliance Review and Critical Management Escalation.

## 2. Mandatory Escalation Triggers

### 2.1 Safety

Any safety hazard is escalated to the Specialist Team (Product Safety) as described in the Product Safety and Recall Policy.

### 2.2 Privacy and Security

Any exposure of camera footage, personal data or unauthorized account access is escalated to Compliance Review.

### 2.3 Legal Threats

A complaint that mentions legal action, a lawyer or a consumer court is escalated to the Department Manager.

### 2.4 Repeated Complaints

A customer complaining for the third time about the same unresolved issue is escalated to Supervisor Review.

### 2.5 High-value Disputes

A dispute involving more than 500 USD is escalated to the Department Manager.

## 3. Escalation Notes

Every escalation must include a complaint summary, key facts, the reason for escalation, actions already taken, the relevant policy and the required next action.
