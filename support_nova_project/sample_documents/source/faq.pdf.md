# Lumora Home Technologies - Frequently Asked Questions

Document ID: FAQ-GEN | Version: 1.0 | Type: FAQ | Status: Active

Effective Date: 2025-06-01

## 1. How do I track my order?

Open the Lumora app, go to Orders and select the order to see the courier tracking link.

## 2. How long do I have to return a device?

You can return a device within 14 days of delivery.

## 3. Are late deliveries compensated?

Yes. Every late delivery receives a 10 USD voucher.

## 4. How do I cancel LumoraCare+?

You can cancel at any time in the Lumora app under Account and Subscription.

## 5. What does the warranty cover?

Lumora devices have a 12-month warranty covering manufacturing faults.

## 6. My device is offline. What should I do?

Update the Lumora app, restart your hub and check that your Wi-Fi network is 2.4 GHz.
