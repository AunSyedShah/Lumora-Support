# Lumora Home Technologies - Compensation Guidelines

Document ID: CMPN-GDL | Version: 1.0 | Type: Policy | Status: Active

Effective Date: 2026-01-01

## 1. Scope

These guidelines define what compensation agents may offer.

## 2. Offering Compensation

### 2.1 Permitted Compensation

Agents may only offer compensation that a policy explicitly allows: full refund, partial refund, shipping fee refund, fee waiver, replacement, repair or subscription credit.

### 2.2 No Discretionary Offers

Agents must not offer vouchers, cash, discounts or gifts that no policy allows, and must not guarantee any outcome before it has been verified.

### 2.3 Goodwill and Exceptions

Goodwill gestures and policy exceptions need Supervisor approval before they are offered to the customer.

## 3. Special Cases

### 3.1 Safety and Property Damage

Compensation for safety incidents or property damage is decided only by the Product Safety team or Management.
